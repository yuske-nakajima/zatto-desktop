A place for the HTML on your desk

Keep your HTML files together.

zatto opens local HTML files in one calm workspace, so reviewing a folder of pages feels like one task instead of a trail of browser tabs.

Workflow

From folder to focused view

  1. 01

    Choose HTML files

    Select one or many .html and .htm files from your local system.

  2. 02

    Drop anywhere

    Drag and drop files across the whole window, including over the active preview.

  3. 03

    Move between pages

    Switch files from one panel while their relative images and styles keep working.

One tool, two ways in

CLI and desktop are the same zatto.

Use the command line when a terminal is already part of your work. Use the desktop app when choosing and dropping files is the natural starting point.
